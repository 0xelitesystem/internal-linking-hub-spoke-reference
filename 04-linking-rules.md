# Linking rules

Link the hub to every spoke, link every spoke back to the hub, link related spokes to each other, and use descriptive link text that tells the reader and the engine what the linked page covers. Keep the links relevant and the anchor text natural.

## Hub to spokes, spokes to hub

The two essential links are the hub linking out to each spoke and each spoke linking back to the hub. The first routes readers and authority to the spokes; the second concentrates authority on the hub and keeps every spoke connected to the center. These reciprocal links are the backbone of the cluster, and every spoke should have its link back to the hub. Without them, the cluster is not actually connected.

## Spoke to spoke

Beyond the hub connections, link related spokes to each other where the relationship is genuine and useful to the reader. A reader on one spoke who would benefit from a related spoke should find a link to it. These lateral links strengthen the cluster's connectivity and help readers move between related depth. Add them where they serve the reader, not everywhere mechanically; relevance is what makes a spoke-to-spoke link valuable.

## Use descriptive anchor text

The text of an internal link should describe what the linked page is about, in natural language, so both the reader and the engine know what to expect. Descriptive anchor text helps the engine understand the linked page's topic and helps the reader decide whether to follow it. Avoid vague link text like generic click-here phrasing, which tells neither the reader nor the engine anything. Let the link text carry the topic of its destination.

## Keep links relevant

Internal links should connect genuinely related pages, not be added indiscriminately to inflate link counts. A link is useful when it leads somewhere relevant to where the reader is; it is noise when it does not. The cluster works because its links reflect real relationships between topics. Keeping links relevant preserves the structure's meaning, both for the reader navigating it and for the engine reading it as a signal of how your content relates. Relevance is the rule that keeps the structure honest.
