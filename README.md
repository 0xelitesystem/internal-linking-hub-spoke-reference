# Internal Linking: Hub and Spoke

A plain-language reference on the hub-and-spoke model of internal linking: organizing your site so a central hub page on a topic links to detailed spoke pages and they link back, building topic authority and helping both readers and search engines understand your site. Written for operators structuring content sites.

## The core idea

The hub-and-spoke model organizes content around topics: a hub page gives a broad overview of a topic and links out to spoke pages that cover its subtopics in depth, while each spoke links back to the hub and to related spokes. This structure concentrates topic authority on the hub, helps readers navigate, and signals to search engines that your site covers the topic thoroughly. It is structure as a discovery and authority strategy.

- `04-linking-rules.md` how to link between hub and spokes.
- `05-common-mistakes.md` the structural errors that waste the model.

## The stance

This reference treats internal linking as a deliberate structure, not an afterthought. Links between your own pages are signals you fully control, and the hub-and-spoke model uses them to build topic authority that scattered, unstructured linking cannot.
