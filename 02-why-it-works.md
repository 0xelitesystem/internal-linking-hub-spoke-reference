# Why it works

The hub-and-spoke model works because it concentrates topic authority, makes your site's coverage of a topic legible to search engines, helps readers navigate to what they need, and distributes link value among related pages. Structure turns a collection of pages into a recognized treatment of a topic.

## Concentrates authority

Internal links pass authority between pages, and the hub-and-spoke pattern channels it deliberately. Spokes link to the hub, concentrating authority on it so it can rank for the broad topic term, while the hub passes authority out to the spokes. This intentional flow builds up the pages you most want to rank, rather than letting link value scatter randomly. The structure directs authority where you want it.

## Signals thorough coverage

A connected cluster of a hub and many spokes signals to search engines that your site covers the topic thoroughly, not just in a single page. Thorough, well-structured coverage of a topic is a strong signal of topic authority. The interlinking makes the breadth and depth of your coverage explicit and discoverable, so the engine can see that you treat the topic as a whole. Structure communicates thoroughness.

## Helps readers navigate

For readers, the model provides a clear map: the hub orients them to the topic and routes them to the depth they want, and the spokes connect to related spokes for further reading. This navigability keeps readers on the site, moving between related pages, finding what they need. A reader who lands on any page in the cluster can easily reach the rest, which serves the reader and signals engagement to the engine.

## Distributes discovery

The interlinking also helps every page in the cluster get discovered and indexed, because each is linked from others rather than orphaned. A spoke linked from the hub and from related spokes is reachable and discoverable; an unlinked page is not. The structure ensures that the authority and discoverability of the cluster lift all its pages, including newer or deeper spokes that would struggle to be found on their own. No page in a well-built cluster is left isolated.
